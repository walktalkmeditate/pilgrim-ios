## Description

Every walk holds something waiting to be noticed.

A walking companion for those who walk with intention, whether across a continent or around the block. No leaderboards. No calorie goals. No social feeds. Just you, the path, and whatever arises.

WALK

GPS tracking with live pace, steps, altitude, and waypoint marking. A three-way breakdown shows how each walk splits between walking, talking, and meditating, because those are different states of attention. Lock the screen and keep going; a Live Activity holds it there.

SEEK

For days you want to be led. Choose a duration, set an intention, and Pilgrim hides clearings in fog on your map: places you didn't choose, shaped by your question and the hour you set out. A quiet pulse guides you: a sonar ping in your pocket, a crescent of light on the map. Reach the fog and be still, and the way continues. Found places return in your summary glowing with the light of the hour you reached them.

HONOR

Follow a path someone else laid down: a walk a friend shared, one of your own walked again, or a stage of the Camino, the Kumano Kodō, or Shikoku's eighty-eight temples. Their voices arrive where they were spoken, and you can answer in the same place. Save the maps before you go and the way holds with no signal at all.

LEAVE A WHISPER

Leave an ephemeral audio message at your location for others to find. Choose from seven energies (presence, lightness, wonder, gratitude, compassion, courage, stillness) and set how long it lingers: a day, a week, a month. Walk near someone else's and it plays softly, a voice from a stranger who passed this way.

PLACE A STONE

Start or grow a cairn as you walk. Cairns are permanent, evolving through seven tiers as pilgrims add stones, from a faint mark to an eternal cairn of 108. Each tier carries its own kanji and meaning. Walk to one to see who has been here before you.

RECORD

Tap to capture a voice note at any moment, timestamped and pinned to your location. After the walk, everything is transcribed on your device using WhisperKit. No audio ever leaves your phone.

GATHER YOUR RELICS

Photos from a walk appear in a carousel on your summary, gathered from Apple Photos by time and GPS. Pin one and it becomes a circular thumbnail anchored to exactly where you stood: on your map, your walk page, and inside .pilgrim exports. Nothing is copied or uploaded, only referenced. Opt-in, default off.

REFLECT

Six styles of writing prompts (contemplative, reflective, creative, gratitude, philosophical, journaling), generated on-device from your own words, walk context, and pinned photos. Copy them into your writing tool of choice and turn a walk into writing.

MEDITATE

A meditation mode with an animated breathing circle and customizable rhythms. Downloadable voice guide packs offer spoken prompts during stillness. Ambient soundscapes accompany the practice. Meditation time is tracked separately, alongside walk time.

PILGRIM COLLECTS GOSHUIN

In Japan, pilgrims collect vermilion ink stamps at temples along a route. Pilgrim generates a digital seal for each walk from its own data: distance, duration, weather, elevation. Milestones press their own seals for a first walk and a first unknown. Your collection grows with your practice.

YOUR WALK IS YOURS ALONE

Everything stays on your device, transcription included. No accounts, no analytics, no behavioral profiling. Delete the app and your data goes with it, unless you exported it first.

An anonymous counter, with no accounts or identifiers, tracks how far pilgrims have walked together and crosses milestones as it goes: the Camino, the Shikoku 88, the length of Japan. Weather awareness via WeatherKit. Moon phases and planetary hours woven into the walk context. Seasonal color shifts calibrated to your hemisphere. Export as .pilgrim packages. Share walks as ephemeral web pages or hand-painted etegami postcards. View your journey at view.pilgrimapp.org.

Open source. Free forever. No subscriptions.

## App Review Notes

Pilgrim uses background location to track walking routes while the screen is locked during walks (30+ minutes). The app also plays ambient soundscapes and meditation voice guides in the background during active walks. Both background modes are only active during an explicit user-initiated walk session and stop when the walk ends.

To test: tap Wander on the Path tab, then tap Start. Walk or let City Run simulation run for a few minutes. Tap Meditate to see the meditation mode. Tap End to complete the walk and see the summary.

To test Honor, the new third mode: tap Honor on the Path tab. "Walk a pilgrimage" lists real routes. Open one (the Kumano Kodō's Nakahechi is the shortest) and tap Download; it fetches a small package of stages from a public dataset. Open a stage, then Begin. "Save maps for the way" on the route page downloads the basemap so the stage renders without a network connection. A shared walk can also be opened by pasting a walk.pilgrimapp.org link into the same sheet. No account is needed for any of it.
